How it works

Step 01
📷
Upload Resistor Image

Provide a clear photo of your resistor with a plain background. PNG, JPG, and JPEG formats accepted. Lay the resistor flat and horizontal for best results.

Step 02
🔬
HSV Color Analysis

OpenCV converts the image to HSV color space and applies per-color masks with Gaussian blur and morphological cleanup to isolate each color band accurately.

Step 03
⚡
Calculate & Display

Detected bands are sorted by x-position and fed into the 4-band or 5-band resistance formula. Results show value and tolerance instantly.
