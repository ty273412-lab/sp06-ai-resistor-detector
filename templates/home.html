How it works

01
📷
Upload Image

Provide a clear photo of your resistor. The system accepts PNG, JPG, and JPEG formats up to 16 MB.

02
🔬
HSV Analysis

OpenCV converts to HSV color space, applies per-color masks with morphological cleanup to isolate each band contour.

03
⚡
Calculate Result

Bands are sorted by x-position. Resistance formula applied for 4 or 5-band resistors. Manual correction available.
