How it works

01
📷
Upload Image

Provide a clear photo of your resistor. The system accepts PNG, JPG, and JPEG formats.

02
🔬
HSV Analysis

OpenCV converts the image to HSV color space and applies per-color masks with morphological cleanup to isolate bands.

03
⚡
Calculate & Output

Detected bands are sorted by x-position and fed into the resistance formula. Manual correction is available.
